01The story

A sound that
won’t sit still.

Since forming in Sacramento in 1988, Deftones have made music that lives in the beautiful tension between force and feeling.

Across ten studio albums, the band have moved between crushing guitar walls, hushed melodies, and widescreen atmosphere—never staying in one lane for long. Their 2000 album White Pony brought a new audience into that world, while later records kept reshaping it.

04What makes it

Loud.
Soft.
Both.

The contrast is the point.

Chino Moreno’s shifting vocals move from intimate whispers to soaring cries. Stephen Carpenter’s guitar brings the weight; the rhythm section locks it to the ground, while Frank Delgado’s samples and keyboards open up strange new space around the edges.

It is music made of opposites: tenderness and distortion, clarity and haze, tension and release.
